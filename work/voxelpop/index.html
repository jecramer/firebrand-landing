Voxelpop: a strategy game for an overlooked audience

Our research at Skunkworks found a group of players that mobile strategy games largely ignored: women. I founded Voxelpop to build for them.

Role
Founder and CEO
Period
2022–2025
Game

Background

Mobile strategy is a big genre, but its games were mostly designed and marketed for men.

The idea

A more approachable strategy game, marketed to the players the genre had overlooked, could reach people the market leaders weren’t reaching.

My role

I founded Voxelpop in 2022 and raised angel investment from games investors including Jon Hook, Hugo Furneaux and a16z’s angel fund, with support from Business Finland. The growth plan helped attract a founding team with almost 60 years of combined experience and more than 60 shipped games.

What we did

Our first game, Goblin Quest, reached a working MVP after ten weeks and was tested on Android in the UK and US. Automated ad production let us test many variations quickly.
